**Two mIoU numbers, two methods.** The **mIoU column below** is the mean over seeds of each seed's own `mean_iou` (`mean_iou_mean` +/- `mean_iou_std` in the JSON beside this file): one value per seed, so it carries a standard deviation like every other column. The **per-class IoU table further down** is recomputed from the **summed** confusion matrix (`iou_per_class_from_summed_cm`, and its own mIoU `mean_iou_from_summed_cm`): the seeds' matrices are added together first and the ratios taken once, which is what training-regiment.md section 1 means by "per-class tables from the seed-averaged confusion matrices". Summing first is the right choice per class -- averaging ratios would weight a seed that saw few examples of a rare class equally with one that saw many -- but it collapses the seed dimension, so that number has **no error bar and cannot be given one**. The two mIoUs therefore disagree slightly (typically in the 4th decimal); neither is wrong, and a paper must not present one as a correction of the other.
